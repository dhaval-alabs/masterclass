CONVERSION TRACKING · OVERVIEW

How We Track & Optimise
Webinar Conversions on Meta

A plain-language explanation of the dual-channel (Pixel + Conversions API) tracking system powering the AnalytixLabs "Excel to AI" Masterclass — and what it delivers for ad performance.
Prepared for Stakeholder review  ·  Platform: Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Ads  ·  Date: 8 June 2026

The one-paragraph summary

Every meaningful action a prospect takes — visiting the page, registering, verifying their phone (OTP), qualifying as a lead, and actually attending the webinar — is reported back to Meta. We send these signals through two independent channels at once: the browser Pixel and Meta's Conversions API (server-side). Both carry a shared ID so Meta counts each conversion once but with the matching strength of both. The result: Meta's AI can find more people like our best prospects — the ones who verify and attend — which lowers cost-per-registration and improves lead quality over time.

Why this matters (the business case)

Lower cost per result

By telling Meta exactly who registered and attended, its algorithm optimises ad delivery toward similar people — not just clicks. Over time this reduces cost-per-registration and wasted spend.

Optimise for quality, not just volume

We don't just send "someone filled a form." We send verified registrations and actual attendance + watch time — so Meta learns to find serious prospects, not tyre-kickers.

Accurate measurement

iOS limits and ad-blockers break browser-only tracking. The server-side channel recovers those lost conversions, so reported numbers stay accurate and ROAS is trustworthy.

Powerful audiences

Because attendance flows to Meta, we can build lookalike audiences of attendees and retarget no-shows — the highest-leverage audiences we own.

How it works — the dual-channel mechanism

A
Browser Pixel (client-side). A small Meta script loads on the website. The instant a prospect registers or verifies, it fires the event straight to Meta from their browser — carrying ad-cookie signals (_fbp / _fbc) that link the action back to the ad they clicked.
B
Conversions API (server-side). At the same moment, our server sends the same event directly to Meta — independent of the browser. This is immune to ad-blockers, browser crashes, and iOS tracking limits. For attendance and lead-scoring (which happen after the user has left), this is the only channel.
C
Shared Event ID = no double counting. Both copies carry one identical ID. Meta recognises them as the same conversion and merges them into one — keeping the report accurate while combining the matching signals from both channels for the best possible accuracy.
D
Privacy by design. Before any personal detail (email, phone, name, city) leaves our system on the server channel, it is irreversibly encrypted (SHA-256 hashed). Meta matches on the encrypted value — it never receives readable personal data. This keeps us aligned with privacy best practice.

Data quality & what Meta receives

"Match quality" = how reliably Meta can connect a conversion to a real ad-clicker. Higher match = better optimisation.

High match

Registration & OTP-verified events — sent on both channels with ad cookies + encrypted contact details.

Good match

Lead qualification — encrypted email & phone from the server, strong identity match.

Solid match

Webinar attendance — encrypted email & phone. (Can be raised further; see roadmap.)

What is shared with Meta (all encrypted)

  • Email, phone, first/last name, city, country — SHA-256 hashed before sending; Meta cannot read the originals.
  • Ad-click cookies (_fbp / _fbc) and the page URL — used purely to match the conversion to the originating ad.
  • Event-specific context — e.g. lead score (hot/warm), and for attendance: watch duration and which webinar session.

What this unlocks next

Attendee Lookalike audiences

Use the WebinarAttended signal to ask Meta for "more people like our attendees" — typically the cheapest, highest-converting prospecting audience.

No-show retargeting & suppression

Optionally emit a "No-show" signal to re-engage drop-offs with the recording, or suppress already-converted users to save budget.

Optimise toward attendance

Shift campaign optimisation from "registration" to "verified attendance," so spend chases the outcome that actually drives revenue.

Even higher match quality

Carry ad-click cookies through to the attendance event to lift its match rate from "solid" to "high."

One dependency to confirm: the server-side relay for Registration & OTP events runs through a tag manager (Stape sGTM). It's worth a quick check in Meta Events Manager that those two events show "Browser + Server" — confirming both channels are live. Attendance & lead-scoring send to the server API directly and are not affected.
